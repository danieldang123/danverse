# Privacy policy - Danverse

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Danverse and its assistant Orbi are a personal project. They are used only by their owner, on the owner's own computer. There are no other users.

**What Google data is used.** Orbi reads the owner's own Google Calendar and creates, changes or removes events only when the owner asks it to.

**Where data is kept.** The Google access token and a log of calendar changes are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Danverse runs no server that stores user data.

**How data is processed.** To understand a request, the text of the owner's request and the relevant calendar details are sent to the AI model that powers Orbi (Claude, by Anthropic). They are not used for advertising.

**Sharing.** Calendar data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

**Limited Use.** Danverse's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

**Removing access.** Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also stops all access.

**Contact.** Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Danverse.

---

Tóm tắt: Danverse là dự án cá nhân, chỉ chủ nhân dùng. Orbi chỉ đọc và sửa lịch Google của chính chủ nhân khi được yêu cầu. Khoá truy cập nằm trên máy tính của chủ nhân, không chia sẻ dữ liệu cho ai. Có thể thu hồi quyền bất cứ lúc nào tại myaccount.google.com/permissions.
